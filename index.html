At a glance

What can I do at the park today?

Al Shaheed Park is Kuwait City's major urban green destination: landscaped gardens, a lake and water features, the Thekra and Habitat museums, walkways and jogging tracks, cafés and restaurants, and community programming through the year. With Phase III it also becomes a full day-out destination.

  • Nature meets skyline

    Landscaped greenery and water features sitting inside central Kuwait City, with the towers as a backdrop.

  • Culture and history

    Thekra and Habitat museums add an educational layer well beyond ordinary park recreation.

  • Genuinely usable fitness

    Long walking and jogging routes make the park practical for a repeatable exercise routine.

  • Food and coffee

    Several cafés and dining concepts let a visit work as a social outing rather than just a walk.

  • Two seasons, two parks

    Winter supports long outdoor stays; summer works early morning or after sunset, with indoor options growing.

  • Programming that changes

    The park has hosted walks, fitness activations, workshops, camps, cultural gatherings and light festivals.

Fitness & active lifestyle

Move Through the Park

The park is especially strong for walking and jogging: public descriptions consistently identify dedicated walkways and jogging tracks, and the park has organised community walking and fitness activities.

  • Walking

    Flat, continuous walkways that suit slower company and strollers.

  • Jogging

    Dedicated tracks, loopable without leaving the greenery.

  • Running

    Long enough for intervals or a steady continuous session.

  • Morning activity

    The coolest, quietest window — the default in summer.

  • Evening movement

    Lit routes and a social atmosphere once the heat drops.

    Group walks and fitness activations appear on the calendar.

    Two very different parks

    The Park by Season

    In Kuwait the season decides the visit. Winter opens the whole park up; summer moves it to the edges of the day and indoors.

    Winter · the park's strongest season

    Winter at Al Shaheed Park

    The big change in 2026

    Phase III

      Before you go

      Visit Information

      Practical detail for planning the trip. Operating details change with the season and the events programme — confirm through the official channels before travelling.

      Location
      Soor Street / Al Soor Gardens, Kuwait City, Kuwait.
      Opening hours
      Currently listed as 5:00 AM – 12:00 AM daily. The Phase II listing shows Friday opening at 1:00 PM. Individual museums, cafés and restaurants keep their own hours.
      Parking
      On-site parking, with 2,388 spaces announced as part of Phase III. On major event nights and weekends, allow extra time for parking and arrival.
      Best visiting times
      Early morning for running and quiet walks; after sunset for families and dining. Winter is the strongest season; in summer, evening is the better leisure window.
      Family information
      Walkways suit strollers, with lawns, water features, museums and activity venues to break a visit into stages. Bring comfortable shoes — the park is best experienced on foot.
      Accessibility
      Paved walkways connect the main areas. If you need step-free access to a specific venue, exhibition or attraction, check with that venue in advance.
      Events & attractions
      Check the park's current social and event calendar for same-day programming, and book Alshaheed Adventures attractions separately. For ticketed events and camps, confirm age and height rules, session length and cancellation terms.
